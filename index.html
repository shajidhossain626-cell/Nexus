Marketing Tips

Ideas we share with every client.

🎣

Hook in the first second

80% of viewers decide whether to watch your ad in under 2 seconds. Lead with pattern interrupts — not brand logos or stock footage.

🔁

Test 3 angles, not 30 variations

Stop testing button colors. Test different messaging angles — pain, aspiration, social proof, urgency — and scale the winning angle.

💰

LTV > CAC isn't enough

Your 60-day LTV matters more than lifetime. If you can't pay back CAC in 60 days, you can't scale fast. Optimize retention first.

🧩

One offer, one audience

Confusion kills conversions. Every landing page should speak to one persona with one promise. Don't try to serve everyone.

📱

Mobile is 90% of ads

Design creatives mobile-first, vertical, captions-on. If it doesn't work at 60% volume on a subway, it doesn't work.

⚡

Speed compounds

A 1-second faster load time = 7% more conversions. Test everything. Kill fast. The compounding effect over 90 days is massive.

🔒 CMS Security

Your CMS is password-protected with SHA-256 encryption. Here's how it works:

  • Password is hashed (encrypted) using SHA-256 - cannot be reversed
  • Only the hash is stored in browser - not the actual password
  • Even if someone inspects browser storage, they only see gibberish
  • ⚙️ button and Ctrl+Shift+E both require password

⚠️ Important: If you forget your password, you cannot recover it. You'll need to export your content, clear browser data, and set a new password.
